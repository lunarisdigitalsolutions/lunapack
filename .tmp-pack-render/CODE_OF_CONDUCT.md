# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Example harassment-free
and welcoming for everyone.

## Our standards

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes,
and focus on what benefits the community. Harassment, discriminatory conduct,
sexualized attention, threats, insults, and publishing private information are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to maintainers@example.com. Maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
