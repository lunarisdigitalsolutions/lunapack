# Product Overview

## Vision

LunaPack helps platform teams distribute reusable engineering standards as
versioned packs instead of repeatedly copying repository foundations.

The long-term product is a governed foundation for documentation, tooling,
security controls, automation, infrastructure guidance, and AI context.

## Users

- Platform teams author and maintain reusable foundations.
- Project teams adopt approved packs and understand their ownership.
- Security and architecture teams distribute controls and guidance.

## Current Boundary

The MVP is a local CLI for discovering, installing, updating, auditing, and
removing packs from local and Git sources. It provides reproducible resolved
state and conservative file ownership so teams can write and evaluate first
packs.

Hosted marketplaces, registry distribution, automatic migration, broader policy
validation, and AI agents are product direction, not current MVP behavior.

## Principles

- Packs are modular and composable.
- Sources are chosen by consumers and recorded in resolved state.
- Lifecycle changes are previewable and safe by default.
- Product growth must preserve reproducibility and clear ownership.
