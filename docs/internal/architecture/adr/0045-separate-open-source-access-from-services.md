# ADR-0045: Separate Open Source Access From Services

## Context and Problem Statement

LunaPack is created and maintained by Lunaris Digital Solutions. Public project
pages need to identify that support without implying that access to LunaPack or
its maintained packs depends on payment, sponsorship, consulting, or training.

## Decision Outcome

Keep LunaPack and maintained packs free and open source. Do not add a GitHub
funding configuration. Link to Lunaris Digital Solutions as the supporting
company and offer consulting and training as optional commercial services that
are separate from software access and community participation.

### Consequences

- Product pages identify the company responsible for sustained development.
- Users can adopt and contribute to LunaPack without purchasing services.
- Commercial support has a clear route without changing project licensing.

### Confirmation

The website company section and footer link to `https://lunaris.digital`, and
maintainer governance records the funding boundary.
